# 4. PROJECT OVERVIEW

Star Player is a PC single-player KBO baseball player-career simulation game.

The player directly controls exactly one baseball player.

The game focuses on experiencing the complete career and life of that player while the broader KBO world progresses through simulation.

The player may begin by:

1. Creating a new fictional player.
2. Selecting an existing KBO player from the loaded datapack.

There is no playable manager or general-manager mode in the initial scope.

The core fantasy is:

"Play the life and career of one baseball player and create your own KBO history."
